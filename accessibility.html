This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA. The site can be used with a keyboard alone, works with screen readers, respects your browser's reduced-motion setting, and meets AA colour contrast throughout.

If you find something you cannot use, email info@neuksynbio.com and we will fix it.

Our events

NEUK seminars are held in step-free, wheelchair-accessible rooms. Catering includes vegetarian options as standard, and we will cover other dietary requirements if you let us know in advance.

We can arrange other adjustments — reserved seating, a quiet space, advance copies of slides, or captioning where the venue supports it. Ask us at least a week before the event and we will do what we can.
