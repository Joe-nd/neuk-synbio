Funders and partners

NEUK events are free to attend. That is possible because of the organisations below, and it is the thing we protect above everything else.

Where the money goes

NEUK has no staff and no overheads. Support pays for the programme directly.

Speaker travel

Travel and accommodation for invited speakers from outside the region.

Catering

The free lunch and drinks that keep people in the room after the talks.

Student bursaries

Travel costs so students from other campuses can attend without being out of pocket.

Sponsor a seminar

Sponsorship is straightforward and the audience is specific: synthetic biology researchers, PhD students and postdocs across five North East universities.

Sponsors are credited on the event poster, in the announcement across our social channels, on the event listing here, and from the podium on the day.

Email us about sponsoring

What sponsorship includes

  • Your logo on the event poster and all promotion
  • An opportunity to speak, if you have relevant science to present
  • Credit on this website for as long as the archive exists
  • Direct access to a room of early-career researchers
  • Introductions to academic groups working in your area

Sponsors do not control programme content. See our statement on independence.
